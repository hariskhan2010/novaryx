Process

How a subscription works.

No proposals and no long contracts. You get a request board and a steady flow of finished work.

Steps

  1. Subscribe

    Pick a plan. You get a private board where every request and delivery lives.

  2. Request

    Write a task, attach a sketch, a doc or a screen recording. Big projects are split into smaller requests.

  3. Receive

    We deliver to your board, your repository and your design files. You review and ask for revisions.

  4. Pause or continue

    Keep going while there's work. Pause when there isn't, and pick up again later.

FAQ

Questions before you subscribe.

What counts as one request?

One clear task, such as a screen, a feature, a logo or a bug fix. Large projects like a full app are split into a series of requests.

Is there a limit on requests?

No. Add as many as you like to your board. We work on them in order, as many at a time as your plan allows.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Code goes into your repository and designs into your accounts.

How does pausing work?

Pause from your billing page when you have no work queued. Unused days stay on your account for when you come back.

What if I only need one project?

Subscribe for a month, finish it, then pause or cancel.
